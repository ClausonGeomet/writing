Measurement and data sources

The bounded primary-source screen distinguished QXRD bias and preparation variability, analytical covariance, physical split heterogeneity, between-unit geology, and mineral chemistry variation. It found no verified ready-to-fit paired raw error dataset within that screen; failed downloads and unknown sample keys remain unknown. It did not establish a universal QXRD error size, detection threshold or field validation.
