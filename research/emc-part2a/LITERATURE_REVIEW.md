For this study, `a=Cm+epsilon` is a sample mass balance on a common analytical basis. Whiten's structural lesson is decisive: if two feasible mineral vectors differ by a null direction of the response and closure operator, assays cannot choose between them. The ambiguity persists if the assay distribution is observed perfectly. Closure adds one equation only when the listed phases genuinely exhaust whole-sample mass. Nonnegative constraints can make some boundary solutions unique even when matrix rank is deficient, so rank and feasible intervals are reported together.

Plant [mass reconciliation](https://www.metso.com/insights/blog/mining-and-metals/mass-balancing-of-concentrator-data/) adjusts uncertain flows and assays under stream conservation; a [mineral-constrained reconciliation paper](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0892687518301870) extends that setting. The present rowwise fixed-`C` weighted LS is narrower: no circuit streams or flow balances are fitted. Neither a residual nor `1-sum(m)` automatically identifies a missing phase. Real geological ratio constraints would need measured support absent from this tiny generic pilot.

### External evidence screen

The [CSIRO Fortescue collection](https://data.csiro.au/collection/csiro:6753) is the strongest bulk-mass lead, but source/licence, phase completeness, chemistry proxy and independent split remain unresolved. A previous related project already exposed a 14/5/4 split and failure; those samples cannot be called new external validation. Mary-Wildman has nine historically paired XRD/XRF specimens but dry-basis and censoring questions. [Cannington data](https://repository.data.auscope.org.au/dataset/cloncurry-metal-multimodal-integrated-mineral-system-characterisation-data) mix TIMA volume percent with surface pXRF, so direct whole-sample mineral mass validation is unjustified. Plant-bin and other leads require joining, rights and support audits. The [screening table](results/dataset_screen.csv) records all candidates. No external cohort was admitted; field validation is pending.
