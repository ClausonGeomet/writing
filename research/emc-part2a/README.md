# Part 2A: frozen baseline feasibility study

This directory tests what fixed mineral chemistry and oxide assays can identify before any proposed order search is evaluated. Read [the plan](EXPERIMENT_PLAN.md), [mathematics](MATHEMATICAL_DESIGN.md), [literature](LITERATURE_REVIEW.md), then [findings](RESEARCH_FINDINGS.md). All assays and mineral quantities are **whole-sample mass fractions**; reported simCoDaR oxide wt% was divided by 100 once, without oxide renormalization. The total-iron channel is `Fe2O3_total`.

The current Part A freeze is [`baseline_freeze.json`](baseline_freeze.json), SHA-256 `7f44da71e04df10378edc38507fd816323b4208e80e81a109cb1d6c0fca57b82`, made 2026-10-26 08:55:02 UTC before Part B results at 08:56:24 UTC. Its 55 references verify. [`freeze_v1`](freeze_v1/) preserves 13 early SLSQP solver failures among 5,400 rows. [`freeze_v2`](freeze_v2/) records a subsequent numerical revision and an **incomplete historical snapshot**: 55/56 references verify, with the original `fixtures/manifest.json` bytes unavailable. Do not describe every historical freeze as fully reproducible. Version 3 uses a stricter stopping tolerance; no outcome was deleted to conceal an error.

The pinned generator is public simCoDaR commit `d9a0b27676a0cb3a4acb300a3f6b2ebc845d205d` (R package version 0.1.0.9000). [`fixtures/source_manifest.json`](fixtures/source_manifest.json) records source, library and RNG details. The [provenance derivative](results/baselines_provenance.csv) adds units and dependency hash to all 5,400 original baseline rows without changing source fields. Its [sidecar](results/baselines_provenance.json) binds source, derivative and freeze hashes. The original results and times remain in [`results/baselines.csv`](results/baselines.csv). Summary and phase tables derive from those results; plots are descriptive synthetic figures. [Dataset screening](results/dataset_screen.csv) admits no new field validation cohort.

The old tester oracle asserted the Part A sign and was wrong against the original contract, so its historical PASS does not establish original-contract compliance. Focused replay passed the amended legacy convention only. Part A `objective` always reports a squared sigma-weighted residual diagnostic at the returned solution, without a one-half factor. For ordinary `pinv` and `nnls` fits, it is not the minimized unweighted objective. No fit or frozen value was changed.
