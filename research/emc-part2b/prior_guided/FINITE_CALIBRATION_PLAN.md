# Preregistered finite-calibration QAR exploratory pilot
Version 1, 27 October 2026. This document is the complete prospective public plan; no pilot generation or results are claimed. User has accepted the previous mathematical checkpoint with conditions and authorized only this bounded pilot. It supersedes the earlier larger-campaign prospect for this pilot alone. The accepted proof, historical results and previous review remain unchanged.

Question: can an independently learned noisy-calibration quadratic prior improve mineral recovery over prior-free constrained least squares, when does it hurt, and what can bulk assay add beyond a calibrated center? This is synthetic, exploratory evidence about a specified model, not empirical geology or external validation. No GA, order search, four-phase study, oracle prior or confirmatory inference is included.

## Mathematical estimator definition
Let U have columns (2,-1,-1)/sqrt(6) and (0,1,-1)/sqrt(2); U'U=I and U'1=0. These are raw-mass tangent coordinates, not logratios. Let F be the common nonnegative closed feasible set, with declared support and optional fixed Quartz. Let ybar be the raw calibration mean, z_i=U'y_i, S_z the sample covariance with denominator n-1, R_z=U' measurement_cov U. Compute D=S_z-R_z and symmetric eigendecomposition D=V diag(d) V'. Define G=V diag(max(d,variance_floor)) V'; T=(1-shrinkage)G+shrinkage trace(G)I/2. Define M=(T+R_z)/n, B=T+M, K=U B^{-1} U'. Return tangent_cov=T, mean_cov=M, precision=K. center is the unique Euclidean projection of ybar onto F, not projection of each calibration observation. Record raw values and eigenvalues before/after flooring and projection distance. No covariance divided by n is substituted for individual geological variance: B contains T plus an approximate mean-estimation contribution. Covariance-parameter uncertainty is NOT integrated. Fixed-Quartz/support constraints may reduce dimension further; restricting a positive tangent penalty to F remains valid, although its floored directions are regularization choices, not estimated geological variation. Negative observations remain in ybar/S_z; closure and support projections are explicit model operations only.

A0 minimizes 0.5(a-Cm)'W(a-Cm) over F, W=assay_cov^{-1}; among all exact minimizers choose smallest Euclidean norm. A1 is exactly the fitted constrained center, not a posterior predictive mean. This is an explicit departure from prospective A1 in EXPERIMENT_PLAN.md. A2 minimizes the A0 loss plus 0.5 strength (m-center)'K(m-center) over the identical F. It is a plug-in quadratic regularizer, not a fully Bayesian analysis. All priors fitted from noisy independent calibration only. No tuning with evaluation truth. For fixed Quartz, C is constant on F: A2=A1 for any positive strength; changing strength cannot identify the split. For variable Quartz the likelihood measures total Ti and Quartz, never the Anatase/Rutile contrast.
## Global solution and numerical policy
Use finite active-face enumeration of the three-phase convex QP, including vertices, edges and full feasible face, equality-constrained stationary candidates and KKT certification. A0 has an analytical minimum-norm tie break: find the assay-optimal feasible Quartz q, then divide total Ti equally between allowed Anatase/Rutile; if only one allowed, assign all to it. Never perturb the objective with a ridge to break ties. A2 is strictly convex on feasible tangent directions at positive strength. KKT residual normalizes stationarity, negative dual and complementarity by max(1,||H||inf*max(1,||m||inf)+||h||inf), with g=Hm-h and independent equality rows. Feasibility tolerance1e-9, normalized KKT1e-8; analytic solution comparison1e-8 absolute/0 relative; matrix symmetry/PSD roundoff1e-12; duplicate-objective comparison1e-12*(1+abs(objective)). Do not loosen tolerances or clip fitted output after failure. Center projection is explicitly part of the estimator, while signed measurement data are never clipped.

## Reporting, correctness gates, and failure policy
For each cohort and arm with errors e_ij=mhat_ij-mtrue_ij, report mass_RMSE=sqrt(sum(e^2)/(40*3)), phase RMSE_j=sqrt(mean(e_j^2)), phase bias_j=mean(e_j), totalTi_RMSE=sqrt(mean((e_A+e_R)^2)), assay_RMSE_observed=sqrt(mean((C mhat-a)^2)) over 40x2 channels, and assay_RMSE_truth=sqrt(mean((C mhat-C mtrue)^2)). Keep assay fit and latent reconstruction distinct. Report max constraint violation, max scaled KKT residual, failures, negative measurement counts, support contradictions against saved truth, projection distance, fitted center, T, M, B eigenvalues/precision, and eigenvalue floor frequency. Center minus held-out cohort truth mean is a noisy retrospective calibration-transfer diagnostic, not a known population bias estimate and never a fit input. Also report center minus independent calibration latent mean to describe measurement/projection effects; neither is a fitted population truth.

Paired cohort contrasts: A2 minus A0 and A2 minus A1 for mass RMSE and both assay RMSE measures (negative favors A2); report means and descriptive SE=sampleSD(d)/sqrt(50), using the 50 cohort differences, not 2000 specimens. Also report across-cohort SD of center coordinates and mean T/M diagonals in physical coordinates U T U' and U M U'. This separates variability of learned centers from within-population variability without claiming covariance-parameter integration. No hypothesis tests, significance labels, confidence/predictive intervals or coverage claims. Scientific benefit has no acceptance threshold: worse priors and no benefit are valid results. No post hoc scenario selection or expansion.

Declared failure policy: schema/domain errors stop execution as contract failures. If any method fails on any specimen in a cohort, all three cohort metric rows and all paired contrasts for that cohort are marked unavailable, with reason and full planned denominator 50. A fit failure makes A1/A2 unavailable for that cohort; A0 may still be saved diagnostically, but primary aggregation remains all-arm unavailable. Report failure rates out of 500 fits and 20,000 predictions per arm, and per cell out of 50 fits and 2000 predictions. Do NOT compute primary means or SE for a cell with any incomplete cohort; partial raw outputs remain available as incomplete diagnostics. All planned 500 cohort triplets must be present before a complete-pilot verdict.

Numerical acceptance: zero generation/fit/solver failures; maximum constraint violation <=1e-9 and scaled KKT residual <=1e-8; fixed-Quartz positive-strength A2 and A1 agree within 1e-8; chemistry conversion <=1e-12; deterministic reproducibility same environment and seeds (numeric atol1e-12, rtol0), and complete schema/seed/hash/leakage/preservation audit. ## Prospective limitations and departures
A1 is a calibrated constrained center instead of the earlier proposed posterior predictive mean; A2 is a plug-in quadratic point estimator on physical-mass simplex measure, with no claim to a normalized Bayesian posterior. Approximate mean uncertainty is added to intrinsic variance, while finite covariance uncertainty remains unintegrated. Floor/shrinkage/projection may induce bias, especially at small n or high measurement noise; all are recorded. There is no empirical-Bayes claim of full uncertainty and no predictive interval/coverage assessment. Known synthetic laboratory noise is assumed, not estimated from truth. There is no chemistry uncertainty, censoring, detection-limit model, support mixture, field validation or multiple-domain campaign. Wrong occurrence is deliberately false hard evidence shared by all arms. Neither changing prior strength on an exact null direction nor obtaining a unique optimum creates assay identification. Separate seeds produce independent cohorts; specimens within a fitted-prior cohort are not independent method comparisons. Fifty cohorts yield pilot precision only.
