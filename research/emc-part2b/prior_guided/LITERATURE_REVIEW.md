Excluded from evidentiary claims: aggregator summaries, reviews returned incidentally, uninspected citations, and proprietary data. The search did not establish primary evidence of GA advantage for this exact free-fraction EMC decoder. This is an evidence limit, not a universal absence claim. No empirical prior calibration or mineral-recovery result is reported here.

Synthesis (our inference): geological calibration supplies information the assay nullspace lacks; sequential chemical bookkeeping supplies a parameterization and possibly imposed restrictions. Fixed-C continuous mass balance with a quadratic prior already has a strong optimization baseline. Sparse mineral measurements, uncertain chemistry and domain errors motivate explicit uncertainty modelling before algorithm complexity. The decisive current no-go rests on the proof, not on absence of a paper or on historical simulation outcomes.
