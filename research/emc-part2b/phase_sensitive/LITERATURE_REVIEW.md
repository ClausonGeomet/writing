Its exact recorded access limitations remain; no fresh full-text access is implied. HSC allocation order, evolutionary optimization and circuit reconciliation are not used to justify this new QAR measurement model. No need to enlarge the literature search into a GA/package campaign.

Synthesis (our inference): phase-specific observations can supply an assay-nullspace direction; replicated bulk tests supply precision and can condition population predictions through learned correlation. Independent measurements and independent evaluation are needed to distinguish direct recovery from transfer. The small mean update is a transparent working approximation, not a literature-established posterior or evidence that k=5 is optimal. Fifty cohorts and budgets0,2,5 are bounded exploratory choices. Realistic instrument bias/detection limits, spatial/nonexchangeable transfer and information-optimal testwork require another decision after these results.
