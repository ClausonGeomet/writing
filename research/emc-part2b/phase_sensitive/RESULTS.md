## Design and estimand

The three whole-sample fractions are Quartz (Q), Anatase (A), and Rutile (R). Bulk SiO2/TiO2 has chemical matrix `C=[[1,0,0],[0,1,1]]`: it measures Q and total Ti phase mass but does not split A from R. The redundant Ti assay repeats the same row with correlated laboratory error; the independent Anatase-sensitive channel uses `h=(0,1,0)`. The phase-specific observation is informative in the missing A/R direction. The R generator used the pinned public simCoDaR source, an isotropic logistic-normal one-domain QAR scenario, and declared Gaussian laboratory errors. This is a synthetic measurement-law exercise; its 0.02 QXRD SD is an assumed proxy, not an empirical instrument calibration.

Four cells each have 50 independent cohorts. Each cohort has 20 calibration, 20 direct, 10 pool, and 30 independent evaluation specimens. Cells 1–4 respectively use accurate, weak, mismatched, and wrong-occurrence priors. Cell 4 is analyzed under both soft (finite prior, full feasible simplex) and hard (A=0 feasible face) assumptions on the same observations. Stage 1 measures direct specimens with bulk, redundant Ti, or the phase channel. Stage 2 buys k=0, 2, or 5 tests on preordered pool specimens; `P` is bulk plus full QXRD, `R` is original plus replicate bulk. A0 fits observations alone, A1 is the learned prior center alone, and A2 fits observations plus a finite prior penalty. Every within-group estimator uses the declared same feasible set. Purchased units use whole-unit leave-one-out for their own prediction. Independent **unmeasured** evaluation specimens have only target bulk and are the primary transfer stratum. No evaluation phase observation is generated.

The preregistered primary contrast is cohort mass-RMSE `A2_P − A2_R` at k=5 on unmeasured evaluation specimens, reported separately by cell/support. Negative values favor phase purchase. Each displayed SE is the sample SD of 50 paired cohort differences divided by √50; specimens, arms, budgets, and the soft/hard reuse are not additional independent replicates. No significance test or directional success threshold was preregistered.

## Direct and measured results

The stage-1 direct cell-1 A0 mean mass RMSE was 0.090938 for bulk, 0.090934 for bulk plus redundant Ti, and 0.016829 for bulk plus independent Anatase channel. A2 means were 0.031945 with bulk and 0.015226 with phase channel. Direct A0_H−A0_T paired differences were -0.074105 (SE 0.001450), -0.075140 (0.001132), -0.076777 (0.001310), and -0.072600 (0.001323) for cells 1–4 soft/full. These direct gains are larger than most independent unmeasured transfer gains because the evaluated target itself receives a phase-sensitive measurement. At k=5 the purchased/LOO measured A2_P−A2_R means were -0.019058, -0.018503, -0.160079, -0.063447, and -0.003148 in cells 1, 2, 3, 4 soft, and 4 hard respectively. They are secondary and must not be pooled with the unmeasured result. [Figure: direct versus transfer](figures/direct_vs_transfer.png) displays their distinct estimands without treating them as one effect.

## Phase, assay, and total-Ti diagnostics

The cell-1 direct A0_B mean phase RMSEs were Q 0.006008, A 0.111522, R 0.111058; A0_H yielded Q 0.006008, A 0.019654, R 0.020617. For cell-3 unmeasured k=5, A2_P phase RMSEs were Q 0.005980, A 0.177227, R 0.177386, and A/R biases were +0.162481/-0.162643. Thus the residual mismatch is chiefly a phase split, not bulk total Ti. Its totalTi RMSE was 0.005980. The same arm's original bulk-assay RMSE was 0.008131 against observed assays and 0.005980 against latent noiseless assays. Cell-1 unmeasured A2_P gives totalTi 0.005889, observed bulk-assay 0.007908, and latent bulk-assay 0.005889. These metrics have distinct targets; a small bulk residual does not certify the A/R split. The source CSVs contain every phase RMSE/bias and both assay definitions, including less favorable cells and arms.

## Information and measurement-consistent bounds

For cell-1 direct full support, the saved information records give raw/closure-augmented/tangent ranks `2/2/1` for bulk and redundant Ti, versus `3/3/2` for the phase channel. Whitened tangent singular values are approximately `(138.013, 0)` for bulk, `(140.705, 0)` for redundant Ti, and `(139.617, 34.949)` for phase. Correlated repetition improves precision along an existing direction but does not identify the null direction. Under hard A=0 support, the effective tangent is one-dimensional by model restriction, not by acquired phase information.

Abundance bounds optimize each mass over the declared feasible set and a marginal 3-SD measurement box. They exclude the prior and are **measurement-consistent envelopes**, not confidence or credible intervals. In cell-1 direct full support, mean Anatase width among 999 nonempty envelopes per design was 0.431842 for bulk, 0.430261 for redundant Ti, and 0.117261 for phase; one envelope per design was infeasible. Across the full final run there were 42,750 bound rows: 41,586 nonempty and 1,164 infeasible envelopes, with zero numerical bound failures. An infeasible box is retained, not dropped or called a solver failure. In the false hard-support cell-4 direct phase design, 844 of 1,000 boxes were infeasible, and the 156 nonempty ones have A width zero by imposed support. See `results/bounds.csv` and `results/information.jsonl`; width means always use nonempty denominators. Prior strength alone cannot narrow these prior-free bounds.

Do not treat prior blocked tables as complete results.

The working mean covariance `M` accounts approximately for finite calibration mean uncertainty, alongside intrinsic `T`; it is an unconstrained plug-in **uncertainty proxy**, not a calibrated posterior. Covariance estimation uncertainty, projection, and raw-mass approximation are not fully propagated. The fifty-cohort SE describes finite synthetic variation under the chosen generator and assumptions. It is not a predictive interval, field coverage estimate, or evidence that k=5 or this method is globally optimal.
