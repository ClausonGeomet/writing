# Experiment plan: held-out comparison to frozen Part 2A

Part B uses the exact six scenarios, three replicates and 30/10/20 train/validation/test IDs frozen by [Part 2A](../emc-part2a/EXPERIMENT_PLAN.md). Evaluation began after the Part A freeze of 2026-10-26 08:55:02 UTC; B result time is 08:56:24 UTC. [`results/manifest.json`](results/manifest.json) binds the freeze SHA-256 `7f44da71e04df10378edc38507fd816323b4208e80e81a109cb1d6c0fca57b82` and result hashes. All quantities are whole-sample mass fractions on the unnormalized oxide basis, with `Fe2O3_total` the iron channel. Search sees `C_fit` and training assays only; mineral truth is reserved for evaluation.

The fixed grid has six search methods (`fixed`, `exhaustive`, `greedy`, `beam`, `random`, `ga`) × two objectives (`row`, `distribution`). Random and GA each use seeds 101–103; deterministic methods run once: 20 configurations per cohort, 360 fits total. The common nonexhaustive cap is 24 proposals, beam width 2, and the maximum expected run is 600 seconds. Fit order and training scales are frozen before validation/test prediction. Report proposals and cache-miss evaluations separately, objective components, wall/CPU time, assay/mineral RMSE, status and unsupported counts. [`results/metrics_provenance.csv`](results/metrics_provenance.csv) carries row units and dependency revision; its [sidecar](results/metrics_provenance.json) binds the immutable original CSV. No best-seed-only result is selected.

Prospective correctness gates: reconstruction replay ≤`1e-10`; nonnegative/closure and component overuse ≤`1e-8`; easy-case phase recovery ≤`1e-6`; paired observational equivalence assay difference ≤`1e-12`; exhaustive training score no worse than any order by more than `1e-10`; zero unexpected failures. Scientific feasibility benefit requires a held-out mineral RMSE reduction ≥`0.01` in at least two of three matched replicates **versus the predeclared Part A comparator** (`cls_eq`, except `missing_phase` uses `cls_le`), with mean assay RMSE increase ≤`0.001`. An auxiliary distribution objective is separately compared with row objective. GA efficiency requires objective equality/better within `1e-10`, fewer actual evaluations and shorter time in at least two of three paired runs versus simpler methods. These are descriptive pilot rules, not significance tests.

The study intentionally has no laboratory error calibration, field paired holdout, coverage simulation, learned chemistry, circuit mass reconciliation or geological constraint. An invalid or unsupported input is retained with status, not silently removed. Three replicates and millisecond search timings cannot establish broadly general performance.
