The [complete saved-results analysis](ALL_SEED_ANALYSIS.md) covers [1,296 paired comparisons](results/all_seed_comparisons.csv), [648 seed-median groups](results/all_seed_medians.csv), and [120 benefit groups](results/all_seed_benefit.csv). It includes every saved random/GA seed 101/102/103, with no best-seed selection. **All 120 B method/objective/seed groups fail** the predeclared benefit rule: mineral RMSE reduction ≥`0.01` in ≥2/3 matched replicates with mean assay RMSE increase ≤`0.001`. No B/A pair reaches the mineral threshold. The Part A reference is always predeclared `cls_eq`, except `missing_phase` uses `cls_le`.

Search can substantially improve the weak lexical fixed order within B: easy-case median search-minus-fixed mineral and assay RMSE are about `−0.173455` and `−0.073394`, and missing-phase medians about `−0.048894` and `−0.009680`. Greedy kept the fixed result in all saved cases; ambiguous orders give identical held-out errors. These within-B gains do not establish B superiority over constrained A. Distribution versus row scoring at the same method and seed usually leaves held-out errors identical. In infeasible cohorts, distribution search can lower mineral RMSE by about `0.03–0.054` per replicate but raises assay RMSE by about `0.034`, beyond the unchanged allowance. The 180 paired Anatase/Rutile swaps have identical assays, so even the complete assay law cannot resolve the chemical null direction.

Across all saved seeds, GA's joint score-within-`1e-10`, strictly fewer evaluations and strictly lower measured fit-time wins versus **beam** are **18/54 row** and **14/54 distribution** pairs. The earlier seed-101-only `0/36` conclusion was a subset, not the full result. Against greedy GA has **0/54** joint wins for each objective; against exhaustive it has **45/54 row** and **42/54 distribution**. The ≥2/3 three-replicate rule passes in only 6/18 row and 5/18 distribution GA-versus-beam scenario/seed groups; [all group verdicts](results/all_seed_ga_efficiency.csv) are retained. GA fit times vary by seed at about 0.0065–0.0262 seconds, so these measurements support no general speed guarantee. Exhaustive is a finite training-objective oracle, not a universal mineral optimum. The [Part A contract amendment](../emc-part2a/CONTRACT_DEVIATIONS.md) records its legacy `reconstructed-a` residual and always sigma-weighted objective diagnostic; Part B retains `a-reconstructed`.

Fresh external validation remains pending. Earlier Fortescue 14/5/4 work was already exposed and its failure is not a newly replicated finding. The numerical record retains early Part A failures, historical v2's missing snapshot reference (55/56) and the complete current freeze (55/55). The findings support a narrower conclusion: chemical information and independently verified phase data matter more than order-search complexity for this pilot.
